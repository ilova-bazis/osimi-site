This document gathers source-backed research for the future `Works` page on Muhammad Osimi. It is intended as a working content dossier before implementation.

Scope:
- authored books and brochures
- major article and essay clusters

- encyclopedia and dictionary projects
- UNESCO and international publication work
- translations from Russian into Tajik
- recommended information architecture for the public page

## Key Findings

- The current `content/works.ts` dramatically understates the amount of available material.
- Osimi should not be presented only as an author of a few philosophy books.
- The corpus supports presenting him across at least six roles:
  - author
  - co-author
  - editor / responsible editor
  - encyclopedia organizer
  - UNESCO project leader / co-editor
  - translator and builder of scientific vocabulary in Tajik
- The current page has one major factual issue:
  - `Tajik Soviet Encyclopedia` is not a `1978`-only item
  - the volumes were published from `1978` through `1988`

## Research Caveats

- Some bibliography files are clean enough to use directly, but some still carry OCR noise.
- Role labels should be handled carefully during implementation:
  - `author`
  - `co-author`
  - `editor`
  - `responsible editor`
  - `scientific editor`
  - `translator`
  - `supervising editor`
  are not interchangeable.
- In several places the corpus preserves both a Tajik original and a later foreign-language or Dari edition of the same work. The public page should decide whether to merge these under one record or show them as separate editions.
- The article corpus is much larger than what the public page should present. The page should favor representative works with short interpretation over exhaustive listing.
