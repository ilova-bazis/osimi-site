For the homepage, I would make it feel **ceremonial, clear, and emotionally grounded**. The job of the homepage is not to tell everything. It should do three things well:

1. introduce Muhammad Osimi with dignity
2. show the breadth of his life and legacy
3. invite the visitor deeper into the biography, works, documentary, and archive

Based on the material you shared, the homepage should present him as a scholar, humanist, science organizer, statesman, and international public figure whose work connected education, science, culture, and peace.  

## Recommended homepage structure

### 1. Hero section

This should be visually strong and simple.

**Left side**

* Name
* One-line identity statement
* Short introduction
* Two primary buttons

**Right side**

* Portrait of Muhammad Osimi
* Optional subtle archival texture or signature background

**Suggested hero copy:**

**Muhammad Osimi**
**Scholar, Humanist, Science Organizer, and Public Figure**

Muhammad Osimi devoted his life to knowledge, education, culture, and the intellectual future of Tajikistan. A physicist by training and a philosopher by depth, he became one of the most important organizers of scientific and cultural life in Central Asia, building institutions, leading scholarship, and advancing dialogue across nations. 

**Primary buttons**

* Explore Biography
* Watch Documentary

**Secondary link**

* View Timeline

### 2. Featured quote band

Right below the hero, I would place one unforgettable quote in large serif type.

Best homepage quote:

**“Men of culture are the Hosts of History, men of politics are the Guests.”**

### 3. Short biography preview

This is a compact section that tells visitors why he matters.

**Suggested copy:**

Born in Khujand in 1920, Muhammad Osimi lived through war, reconstruction, nation-building, and intellectual transformation. He served in World War II, became the first rector of the Tajik State Polytechnic Institute, held senior public leadership roles, and led the Academy of Sciences of Tajikistan for more than two decades. His work crossed disciplines and borders, linking philosophy, science, education, history, and culture into one enduring legacy. 

**Button:**

* Read Full Biography

## Homepage tone

The homepage should feel:

* dignified
* warm
* scholarly
* visually calm
* archival, but not dusty
* international, but rooted in Tajik history
