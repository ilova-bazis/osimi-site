# Osimi Fact Conflicts

This note tracks source conflicts and temporary editorial decisions for Muhammad Osimi / Muhammad Saifiddinovich Asimov content across the site.

## Current source priority

Until a fuller vetted source packet is prepared, use the following order:

1. `assets/biography-en.txt` as the primary working source.
2. `assets/biography-tj-short.txt`, `assets/biography-tj.txt`, and `assets/biography-ru.txt` as supporting internal sources.
3. External scholarly sources, especially Encyclopaedia Iranica, as conflict markers and secondary checks.

This is a temporary editorial policy, not a claim that the internal English file is the final historical authority.

## Facts currently treated as stable

- Muhammad Osimi / Muhammad Saifiddinovich Asimov was a major Tajik scholar, educator, public figure, and long-serving president of the Academy of Sciences of the Tajik SSR / Tajikistan.
- He studied in Khujand and Samarkand, graduated in physics in 1941, fought in World War II near Leningrad, and later held major academic, political, and UNESCO-related roles.
- He served as president of the Academy of Sciences from 1965 to 1988.
- He was associated with the International Jawaharlal Nehru Prize in 1983.

## Fact conflicts to revisit

### 1. Birth date

- Internal site source: `assets/biography-en.txt:3` and `assets/biography-en.txt:105` give `August 25, 1920`.
- Supporting internal Tajik sources also use `25 August 1920`.
- External source: Encyclopaedia Iranica gives `1 September 1920`.

Current site policy:

- Use `25 August 1920` for now because it is repeated across the current internal source set.

Future action:

- Re-evaluate once a stronger primary-source packet is available.

### 2. Death date

- Current internal source set used in this pass does not clearly establish an exact day and month in the provided excerpts.
- External source: Encyclopaedia Iranica gives `29 July 1996`.
- Earlier site/source material had also produced a conflicting `27 September 1996` reference.

Current site policy:

- Use `died in 1996` rather than a precise date.

Future action:

- Adopt an exact death date only after it is confirmed by the future vetted source packet.

### 3. Jawaharlal Nehru Prize chronology

- Internal prose sources: `assets/biography-en.txt:31` and `assets/biography-en.txt:73` present the prize as `1983`.
- Internal chronology source: `assets/biography-en.txt:328` says `1979` laureate, with the award ceremony in Moscow in `1983`.
- External source: Encyclopaedia Iranica says he was honored with the award in `1983`.

Current site policy:

- Use the public-facing wording `laureate of / honored with the International Jawaharlal Nehru Prize (1983)`.
- Avoid more detailed ceremony chronology on public pages unless a later source review confirms the full sequence.

### 4. Tajik Technical University naming

- Internal source: `assets/biography-en.txt:7` supports the present-day wording that the current Tajik Technical University bears his name.
- External source: Encyclopaedia Iranica says the university was named after him posthumously.

Current site policy:

- Use neutral phrasing such as `the Tajik Technical University named after M.S. Osimi`.
- Avoid explicit claims about the exact year or whether the naming occurred during his lifetime unless a stronger source packet confirms it.

### 5. War award chronology

- Internal source clearly supports:
  - medal `For the Defense of Leningrad` in `1944`
  - medal `For Victory over Germany` in `1945`
  - another Order of the Patriotic War in `1985`
- The exact dating of the earlier Order of the Patriotic War is not fully clean across the current internal materials.

Current site policy:

- Keep careful phrasing that dates the medals clearly and mentions the Order of the Patriotic War without overcommitting to a disputed year.
